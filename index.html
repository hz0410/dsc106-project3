    Overview

    This visualization uses CMIP6 aerosol optical depth at 550 nm (od550aer) aggregated by country for each month in 1850. Aerosol optical depth measures how much airborne particles such as dust, smoke, and pollution block sunlight.

    Use the slider to move through the months of 1850. Hover over a country to see its exact value, or select a country to view its monthly trend.
